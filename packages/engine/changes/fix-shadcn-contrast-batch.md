The shadcn/ui registry item draws the box-shadow focus ring at full strength (#4386, owner decisions 2026-11-24 A on #4386 and #4575). Stock shadcn/ui draws every component's focus ring in `ring-ring/50`, 2.0–2.8:1 against the page on the hosted brands, through eight class forms: `focus-visible:` (Button, Input, Select and most controls), the Slider thumb, the Calendar and its focused day, the Input OTP slot, `focus-within:` (Combobox, Attachment), Input Group and Bubble. A second rule in the item's `css` sets each to the full `--ring`, 3.7:1 or more; a ring on an invalid field, flagged on the field or on a field inside it, keeps its stock `destructive` color. The item also gains a `docs` line pointing to the consumer note in the studio README. The plain `shadcn/<brand>.css` is unchanged. `lint-shadcn-contrast` now measures the ring in every form with the rule applied, holds both invalid cases to `destructive`, and measures two faded text pairs at their stock alpha: the destructive Alert's `text-destructive/90` on `card` (#4387) and the dark placeholder on `input/30` over `background` (#4388). Both are recorded as known failures (owner decision 2026-11-24 C), and the studio README gives the one class edit that raises each.
